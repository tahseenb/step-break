Step Break Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Step Break does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything it does happens on your Mac.

What the app stores

Only your settings (days, hours, reminder frequency, and similar preferences). They are saved locally on your Mac in the app's private sandbox container and are never sent anywhere.

No network access

Step Break runs in the macOS App Sandbox without network permission. It cannot connect to the internet, and it contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind.

Smart timing features

  • Wait until calls end: when a reminder is due, the app asks macOS whether another app is currently using a microphone. It never opens the microphone, never records or listens to audio, and does not request microphone permission.
  • Skip if I've been away: the app uses standard macOS signals (screen lock, sleep, and how long since the last keyboard or mouse input) to tell whether you've been away. It does not record what you type or click.

The results of these checks are used immediately to decide whether to show a reminder, and are not saved. Both features can be turned off in Settings.

Tips and support

Tips in the Mac App Store version are processed entirely by Apple through in‑app purchase; Step Break never sees your payment details or identity. In the direct‑download version, "Support Step Break" simply opens a web page in your browser; anything you do there is covered by that site's privacy policy.

Children

Step Break collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
